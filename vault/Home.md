# Frontier Radar

The radar writes here every morning. Your own notes outside the `radar:auto` markers are never overwritten.

- **Today's brief** → newest note in `Daily/`
- **Frontier feed** → ![[Dashboards/Frontier Feed.base]]

## Dashboards
- [[Dashboards/Frontier Feed.base|Frontier Feed]]: everything scored, newest and highest first
- [[Dashboards/Outside the Big Labs.base|Outside the Big Labs]]: high periphery, high score
- [[Dashboards/By Modality.base|By Modality]]: grouped by modality
- [[Dashboards/Needs Rating.base|Needs Rating]]: judged items you haven't rated (ratings train the scorer)
- [[Dashboards/Watchlist.base|Watchlist]]: the people and orgs the radar follows

## How to teach it
Set `rating:` on any item to `frontier`, `solid`, `incremental`, `hype` or `irrelevant`. The next run picks it up,
and the weekly calibration re-fits the scorer.

## Maps
`Maps/` holds a daily JSON Canvas of the top items and the people/orgs behind them.
